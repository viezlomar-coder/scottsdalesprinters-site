Quick answers: 56-Passenger Motorcoach

How many people does the 56-Passenger Motorcoach seat? The 56-Passenger Motorcoach from Scottsdale Sprinters seats up to 56 guests.

How much does the 56-Passenger Motorcoach cost? Scottsdale Sprinters charters the 56-Passenger Motorcoach at $325 an hour with a 5-hour minimum. The shortest booking is $1,625 before 8% Arizona sales tax.

How much luggage fits in the 56-Passenger Motorcoach? The 56-Passenger Motorcoach from Scottsdale Sprinters has room for 125 bags.

Does the 56-Passenger Motorcoach do airport pickups? Yes: Scottsdale Sprinters books the 56-Passenger Motorcoach as a one-way airport transfer from $1,250 before 8% Arizona sales tax. Scottsdale Sprinters holds the Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) ground transportation permit.

Can we drink on board the 56-Passenger Motorcoach? On the 56-Passenger Motorcoach, Scottsdale Sprinters applies its house rule on alcohol. Passengers 21 and over bring their own alcohol, and we do not sell it. Arizona vehicle-for-hire open-container policy, A.R.S. § 4-251, allows it, and if anyone under 21 rides, no alcohol comes on that trip.

What is the 56-Passenger Motorcoach best for? Scottsdale Sprinters books the 56-Passenger Motorcoach for big weddings, big events, team and athlete travel, school and college groups, and moving any large group as one, for up to fifty-six.
