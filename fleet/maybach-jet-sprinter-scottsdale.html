Quick answers: Maybach Jet Sprinter

How many people does the Maybach Jet Sprinter seat? The Maybach Jet Sprinter from Scottsdale Sprinters seats up to 9 guests.

How much does the Maybach Jet Sprinter cost? Scottsdale Sprinters charters the Maybach Jet Sprinter at $295 an hour with a 4-hour minimum. The shortest booking is $1,180 before 8% Arizona sales tax.

How much luggage fits in the Maybach Jet Sprinter? Luggage on the Maybach Jet Sprinter from Scottsdale Sprinters: 4 checked bags and 8 carry-ons.

Does the Maybach Jet Sprinter do airport pickups? Yes: Scottsdale Sprinters books the Maybach Jet Sprinter as a one-way airport transfer from $550 before 8% Arizona sales tax. Scottsdale Sprinters holds the Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) ground transportation permit.

Can we drink on board the Maybach Jet Sprinter? On the Maybach Jet Sprinter, Scottsdale Sprinters applies its house rule on alcohol. Passengers 21 and over bring their own alcohol, and we do not sell it. Arizona vehicle-for-hire open-container policy, A.R.S. § 4-251, allows it, and if anyone under 21 rides, no alcohol comes on that trip.

What is the Maybach Jet Sprinter best for? Scottsdale Sprinters books the Maybach Jet Sprinter for CEOs, small executive groups, celebrity transport, and parties that want the best of the best.
