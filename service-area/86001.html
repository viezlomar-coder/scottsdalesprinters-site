Neighborhood guide

Downtown Flagstaff preserves its Route 66 heritage with historic buildings housing local shops, restaurants, and breweries along San Francisco Street and the surrounding blocks. Northern Arizona University brings collegiate energy to the south side. The train depot and Visitor Center anchor Heritage Square. Winter brings skiers to Arizona Snowbowl, while summer offers hiking in the Coconino National Forest. Lowell Observatory adds stargazing opportunities in the world's first International Dark Sky City.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Flagstaff from Phoenix?

Flagstaff is approximately 145 miles north of Phoenix via I-17. Our comfortable vehicles make the scenic mountain drive relaxing, gaining 6,000 feet in elevation along the way.

Do you provide Grand Canyon transportation from Flagstaff?

Yes! Flagstaff is the closest major city to Grand Canyon South Rim (80 miles). We offer Grand Canyon day trips and transfers, perfect for visitors staying in Flagstaff or traveling from Phoenix.

Can you take skiers to the San Francisco Peaks?

Absolutely! We provide ski season transportation to the ski area in the peaks north of Flagstaff. Our vehicles accommodate ski and snowboard equipment for groups of all sizes.

Do you offer brewery tours in Flagstaff?

Flagstaff has a thriving craft beer scene across its historic downtown. We offer brewery tour transportation so you can safely sample the mountain town's taprooms without driving between them.
