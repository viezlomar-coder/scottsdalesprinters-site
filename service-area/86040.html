Neighborhood guide

Page sits on the southern shore of Lake Powell, the second-largest reservoir in the United States when full. The town serves as base camp for Antelope Canyon tours, which must be visited with authorized Navajo guides. Horseshoe Bend's famous overlook sits just outside town. Glen Canyon Dam offers tours and lake access. Lake Powell itself provides houseboating, kayaking, and fishing opportunities. The Navajo Nation surrounds Page, adding cultural depth to visits.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Page from Phoenix?

Page is approximately 275 miles from Phoenix. Our comfortable transportation allows you to rest while we handle the journey through Arizona's diverse landscapes.

Do you provide Antelope Canyon tour transportation?

Yes! We provide transportation to Page for Antelope Canyon experiences. Note that canyon tours require separate reservations with Navajo-authorized guides. We coordinate timing to ensure you arrive for your scheduled tour.

Can you take me to Horseshoe Bend?

Absolutely! Horseshoe Bend is one of Arizona's most iconic viewpoints. The overlook is a short 1.5-mile round-trip walk from the parking area. We provide comfortable transportation and wait while you experience this stunning view.

Do you offer multi-day Page/Lake Powell trips?

Yes, we offer multi-day adventures combining Antelope Canyon, Horseshoe Bend, Lake Powell, and Glen Canyon Dam. We can also combine Page with Grand Canyon or Monument Valley for comprehensive Northern Arizona experiences.
