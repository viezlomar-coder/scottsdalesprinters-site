Neighborhood guide

East Flagstaff extends along I-40 toward Winslow, providing access to national monuments and natural attractions. Walnut Canyon National Monument preserves cliff dwellings in a beautiful forested canyon. Further east, Wupatki National Monument features multi-story pueblo ruins. Sunset Crater Volcano offers hiking among volcanic cinders. Meteor Crater, the world's best-preserved impact crater, lies an hour east. The area combines outdoor recreation with archaeological exploration.

Frequently asked questions

Can you provide transportation to Meteor Crater?

Yes! Meteor Crater is approximately 45 miles east of Flagstaff. We provide comfortable transportation to this remarkable natural wonder, allowing you to explore the visitor center and crater rim without driving.

Do you offer tours to Walnut Canyon and Wupatki?

We provide transportation to both Walnut Canyon and Wupatki National Monuments. These ancient sites offer fascinating glimpses into Arizona's prehistoric cultures. We can arrange visits to one or both monuments.

How can I visit Sunset Crater Volcano?

Sunset Crater is 15 miles north of Flagstaff via Highway 89. Our transportation service provides comfortable access to the volcanic landscape, often combined with Wupatki National Monument on the same scenic loop road.
