Neighborhood guide

Arcadia blends the charm of established Phoenix with contemporary energy. The neighborhood is famous for its 1950s-era ranch homes on large lots, many now extensively renovated or replaced with modern estates. Citrus groves give way to trendy restaurants and boutiques along Camelback Road. The area is divided into Arcadia Proper (south of Camelback) and Arcadia Lite (north), both highly desirable. Camelback Mountain trails are easily accessible, making morning hikes followed by brunch a local tradition.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide transportation to Arcadia restaurants and bars?

Yes! Arcadia has become a culinary destination. We offer dinner transportation along the Arcadia restaurant row and the 40th Street and Indian School corridors. Enjoy your evening without worrying about parking or driving.

How close is Arcadia to Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport?

Arcadia (85018) is just 8 miles from Phoenix Sky Harbor Airport. Our airport transfer service provides convenient pickup and drop-off for Arcadia residents and visitors.

Can you provide hiking shuttle service to Camelback Mountain?

We offer hiking shuttles to the Camelback Mountain trailheads. Trailhead rules are set by the operators there, not by us, so the written confirmation names the approved drop-off point. We'll drop you off refreshed and pick you up after your hike. Popular with visitors staying in the area who want to experience Camelback without parking hassles.

Do you serve the Biltmore area from Arcadia?

Absolutely! We provide seamless transportation between Arcadia and the Biltmore area, including its shopping, resort and restaurant addresses along the Camelback Corridor.
