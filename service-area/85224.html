Neighborhood guide

West Chandler balances high-tech industry with small-town character. Downtown Chandler has been transformed into a vibrant destination with craft breweries, farm-to-table restaurants, and boutique shopping along San Marcos Place. The Price Corridor hosts major employers including Intel, PayPal, and numerous tech companies. Chandler Fashion Center provides regional shopping. Tumbleweed Park offers recreation. The area attracts young professionals and families with excellent schools and amenities.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide corporate transportation along the Price Corridor?

Yes, we specialize in corporate transportation along the Price Corridor and the Ocotillo tech campuses, with executive car service, airport transfers for visiting executives, and group shuttles.

Can you take me to Downtown Chandler restaurants and breweries?

Absolutely! Downtown Chandler has become a dining and craft beer destination. We provide transportation across Historic Downtown and the Arizona Avenue blocks around it. Enjoy your evening without worrying about parking.

How far is Chandler from Phoenix airports?

West Chandler (85224) is approximately 15 miles from Phoenix-Mesa Gateway Airport. Both Gateway and Phoenix Sky Harbor are convenient for Chandler residents and visitors.

Do you offer Chandler shopping transportation?

Yes, we provide drop-off and pickup service at the West Chandler shopping district. Perfect for visitors who want to shop without a rental car or locals planning a shopping day.
