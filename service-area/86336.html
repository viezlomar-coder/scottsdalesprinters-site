Neighborhood guide

Sedona is divided into distinct areas: Uptown Sedona offers galleries, restaurants, and tourist shops along the main corridor. West Sedona provides a more local feel with dining and shopping. The Village of Oak Creek features Bell Rock views and access to popular trails. Tlaquepaque Arts & Shopping Village recreates a traditional Mexican village with artisan galleries. Oak Creek Canyon to the north offers dramatic scenery and swimming holes. The town's unique energy draws spiritual seekers, artists, hikers, and those seeking rejuvenation.
