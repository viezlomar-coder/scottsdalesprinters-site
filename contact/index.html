Hours

Book online any hour. Quotes answered 8am–8pm Arizona time, seven days. Booked after 8pm? We confirm by 8am. Rides booked during desk hours (8am–8pm) are confirmed right away.

How far ahead to book

Availability varies by date and by vehicle. Same-day bookings are welcome with no cutoff, subject to availability. Request a current quote for your date and we confirm in writing what is open.

What to include in a quote request

The date, the pickup time and address, where you are going, how many passengers, and roughly how many hours you need. That is enough for a firm quote at the published hourly rate; anything else we can settle on the phone.

Where we pick up

Scottsdale, Phoenix, Sky Harbor and the wider Valley: Scottsdale, Phoenix, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Cave Creek, San Tan Valley, and day trips anywhere in Arizona.

How a booking is confirmed

How the rest settles depends on how you pay: on a card, a 50% deposit holds the date and your written confirmation sets out the balance and its timing; paying in full at checkout leaves nothing else due before your ride; a payment link from the reservations desk opens that same checkout. Arizona sales tax is added at checkout; there is no booking fee and no service charge.
