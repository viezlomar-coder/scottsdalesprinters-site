Frequently asked questions

Which breweries do you recommend?

Choose venues from their current official listings, confirm operating hours and reservations directly, and send the final list for route review. Keeping stops in one area usually makes the transportation plan more practical.

Can we mix breweries and distilleries?

A mixed route can be requested. Book tastings directly with currently operating venues, then submit the confirmed stops and timing for transportation review.

Do breweries charge for tastings?

Tasting prices, admission rules, and reservation policies are set by each venue and can change. Check and pay the venues directly unless a written quote expressly includes a listed item.
