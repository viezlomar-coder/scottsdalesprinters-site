Brewery & Distillery Tasting Tour

Scottsdale Sprinters is a Scottsdale chauffeured transportation company serving metro Phoenix. Ride with a professional chauffeur to two or three currently operating breweries or distilleries in one area. Book any required reservations and tastings directly with each venue, then send the confirmed stops for transportation review. Tastings are purchased by guests at each stop and are not included in the transportation price.

Frequently asked questions

Which stops can we request?

Three currently operating examples (checked on their own websites on 15 November 2026) are Goldwater Brewing Co. in Old Town Scottsdale, Fate Brewing Company on Scottsdale Road, and O.H.S.O. Brewery + Distillery in North Scottsdale. We do not partner with these venues. Choose two or three stops in one area, confirm hours and reservations directly, and send the final list for route review.

Are tastings included?

No. Tasting prices, admission rules and reservation policies are set by each venue and can change. Guests book and pay the venues directly unless a written quote expressly includes a listed item.

Can we mix breweries and distilleries?

Yes. A mixed tasting route can be requested. Keep the stops in one area when you can, book tastings directly, and submit the confirmed stops and timing for transportation review.

Is there a drinking age?

Guests who drink must be 21 or older. Please drink responsibly. The transportation price does not include alcohol or tastings.
