Frequently asked questions

What if our flight is delayed?

Provide airline and flight number in the booking. How delays and wait time are handled is stated in your written quote or confirmation — ask reservations to spell that out for your trip.

Which airports do you serve?

Phoenix Sky Harbor and Scottsdale Airport are the core arrivals for this experience. Gateway (AZA) has its own service page. Share the airport code and meeting needs when you request a quote.

Can we book a round-trip airport transfer?

Yes when both legs are on the reservation. Share arrival and departure flight details plus both addresses. Confirm the total in live checkout on the airport service page.

Should we book an SUV or a Sprinter for PHX?

Smaller parties often choose the Premium SUV; groups needing more cabin space for people and luggage typically choose an Executive Sprinter. Statement arrivals can look at the Maybach Jet. Share passenger count and luggage notes, then compare units on the airport service and fleet pages — confirm the total in checkout.

Where does the chauffeur meet us at Sky Harbor?

Your written confirmation names the approved meeting point or procedure. The chauffeur’s message after landing and the airport’s posted signs complete the handoff. Terminal procedures change — do not rely on a door number from an old blog post.
