Frequently asked questions

How much is a limo from PHX to North Scottsdale?

A one-way transfer from Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) to North Scottsdale starts from the flat one-way basis: $225 in the Luxury SUV (Suburban) (up to 6 guests) or $295 in an Executive Sprinter (up to 14). The flat figure covers a transfer within 15 road miles from pickup to drop-off; a longer transfer is priced from the drive with the empty return included, and the checkout shows the exact figure once both addresses are entered. Every transfer includes flight tracking and a professional chauffeur; prices are before tax.

What is the best car service for the Waste Management Open?

For the WM Phoenix Open, Scottsdale Sprinters recommends the Luxury SUV (Suburban) ($125/hour) for up to 6 or the Executive Black Sprinter ($145/hour) for groups up to 14. Both carry bottled water and a professional chauffeur, and both have a 4-hour minimum on an hourly charter. Availability varies by date and by vehicle: request a current quote for your date and we confirm in writing what is open.

Do you offer hourly chauffeur services in Paradise Valley?

Yes: Scottsdale Sprinters offers hourly chauffeur service throughout Paradise Valley, Scottsdale, and the greater Phoenix area. Rates start at $125/hour for the Luxury SUV (Suburban), $145/hour for Executive Sprinters, and $175/hour for Limo Party Sprinters. Minimum 4-hour booking; 7 or more hours earns a free hour. Perfect for dinner dates, shopping trips, or multi-stop errands with your personal driver.

How much does a party bus cost in Scottsdale?

A party bus from Scottsdale Sprinters costs $250/hour in the Party Bus (28 Pax), with a 4-hour minimum ($1,000 for 4 hours, before tax), and the Limo Party Sprinters start at $175/hour on the same minimum. On board: fiber optic lights, two wet bars, best-in-class subwoofers and sound. The rules for alcohol are in our Arizona party bus alcohol guide. Suited to Old Town Scottsdale nightlife, bachelor and bachelorette parties, and birthday celebrations.

Can I book a Sprinter van for a Sedona wine tour?

Yes: the Scottsdale Sprinters Executive Black Sprinter is suited to Sedona and Verde Valley wine tours. The 14-passenger van is $145/hour with a 4-hour minimum, and bookings of 7 or more hours include a free hour, so a full-day wine itinerary is priced at the hourly rate less the free hours. Our Verde Valley wine tour page lays out the day stop by stop, and a written quote confirms the total.

Do you provide wedding transportation in Paradise Valley?

Yes: Scottsdale Sprinters provides Paradise Valley and Scottsdale wedding transportation: bridal-party shuttles, guest transportation and the couple's car. One pairing that works is the Luxury SUV (Suburban) for the couple ($125/hour) with an Executive Sprinter for the bridal party ($145/hour), each on a 4-hour minimum. Champagne cups and ice service and Just Married signage are add-ons you pick at checkout.

How early should I book for a golf tournament pickup?

How early depends on the date rather than on a rule of thumb. Availability varies by date and by vehicle, for a major tournament like the WM Phoenix Open, Schwab Cup or Charles Schwab Challenge as much as for a regular tee time. Request a current quote for your date and we confirm in writing what is open. Golf days run by the hour, with room for the clubs, multiple course stops and a flexible return after the round.

What's the cheapest airport transfer from Sky Harbor?

The most affordable Sky Harbor (PHX) transfer from Scottsdale Sprinters is the Luxury SUV (Suburban) at $225 one way: flat within 15 road miles from pickup to drop-off, and priced from the drive beyond that with the empty return included, before tax. This includes flight tracking and bottled water. For groups of 7-14, an Executive Sprinter at $295 is more cost-effective per person.
