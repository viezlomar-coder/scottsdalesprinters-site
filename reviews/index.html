About Scottsdale Sprinters

Scottsdale Sprinters, also known as Scottsdale Sprinters Buses and Limos, is a chauffeured transportation company in Scottsdale, Arizona. Chauffeured Sprinter vans come first. The fleet includes: Vehicles range from luxury SUVs to Sprinters, limos, party buses, mini coaches and 56-passenger motorcoaches, professional chauffeur service included, and groups of 1,000 to 2,000 guests move on coordinated multi-vehicle service. Book by the hour, or as an airport or point-to-point transfer.

Service area: Scottsdale, Phoenix, Paradise Valley, Tempe, Mesa, Chandler, Gilbert, Glendale, Cave Creek, San Tan Valley and across Arizona.
