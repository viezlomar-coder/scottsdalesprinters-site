What to Ask Before Booking a Limo

Not all limousine companies are created equal. These essential questions protect you from disappointment and ensure a premium experience.

Critical Questions to Ask

1. Licensing & Insurance

"Are you properly licensed and insured?"

What to confirm:

  • Arizona TCP (Transportation Company Permit)
  • Commercial vehicle insurance (minimum $1.5M liability)
  • Workers' compensation coverage
  • DOT compliance for larger vehicles

Red flag: Hesitation to provide documentation

2. Vehicle Age & Condition

"How old is the vehicle I'll be riding in?"

Industry standards:

  • SUVs: Under 5 years
  • Stretch limos: Under 8-10 years (well-maintained)
  • Party buses: Under 10 years

Red flag: Won't let you inspect vehicle beforehand

3. Total Pricing

"What is the complete cost including all fees?"

Breakdown should include:

  • Hourly rate or flat fee
  • Minimum hours required
  • Fuel surcharge (we charge none)
  • Gratuity (ours is optional and never added automatically)
  • Tolls and parking fees
  • Overtime rate

Red flag: Price seems too good to be true

4. Driver Information

"Tell me about your drivers."

Expect:

  • Background checked
  • Drug tested
  • Professionally trained
  • Years of experience
  • Local knowledge

Red flag: No driver assignment until day-of

5. Backup Plans

"What happens if the vehicle breaks down?"

Professional response:

  • Backup vehicle on standby
  • Immediate dispatch capability
  • Client notification protocols
  • Alternative arrangements

Red flag: "That never happens"

Secondary Questions

6. Cancellation Policy

  • How far in advance for full refund?
  • Partial refund windows?
  • Weather-related exceptions?

7. Contract Terms

  • Written agreement provided?
  • What happens if you go overtime?
  • Payment schedule?

8. Amenities Included

  • Bottled water?
  • Ice and glasses?
  • Phone chargers?

9. Special Requests

  • Can you accommodate decorations?
  • Specific music requests?
  • Route customizations?

10. Reviews & References

  • Google reviews rating?
  • Wedding planner recommendations?
  • Length of time in business?

Price Comparison Tips

Apples-to-Apples

When comparing quotes:

  1. Same vehicle type and age
  2. Same number of hours
  3. All fees included
  4. Same service level

What Affects Price

  • Vehicle type and size
  • Day of week
  • Time of year
  • Duration of service
  • Distance traveled
