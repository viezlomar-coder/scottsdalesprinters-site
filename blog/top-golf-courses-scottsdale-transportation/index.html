Top Golf Courses in Scottsdale & How to Get There in Style

Scottsdale Sprinters is a Scottsdale chauffeured transportation company serving metro Phoenix. Scottsdale is the golf capital of the Southwest. Experience world-class courses with luxury transportation that matches the quality of your round.

Premier Scottsdale Golf Courses

TPC Scottsdale

Home of the WM Phoenix Open, featuring the famous Stadium Course with its legendary 16th hole. The Champions Course offers a more traditional experience.

Troon North

Perched in the Sonoran Desert foothills, Troon North's Monument and Pinnacle courses offer stunning views and challenging play.

Grayhawk Golf Club

Two 18-hole courses—Talon and Raptor—known for immaculate conditioning and the lively Phil's Grill 19th hole.

We-Ko-Pa Golf Club

Located on Fort McDowell Yavapai Nation land, Cholla and Saguaro courses showcase authentic Sonoran Desert golf.
