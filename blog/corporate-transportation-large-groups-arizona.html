Vehicle Options for Large Groups

  • Reclining leather seats, each one reclining on its own

Best for: Airport transfers, short shuttles

Best for: Executive groups, VIP transportation

  • Premium interior
  • Privacy
  • Flexible scheduling

Fleet Planning Formula

For events requiring continuous shuttles:

  • Calculate total attendees
  • Determine peak arrival/departure windows
  • Plan for 15-20% buffer capacity
  • Schedule vehicles in 30-minute intervals

Example: 200 attendees over 2 hours. We quote this as coordinated multi-vehicle service — the 56-Passenger Motorcoach with Executive Mini Coaches and Sprinters, in rotations. The exact combination depends on availability on your date and is confirmed in the written quote.
