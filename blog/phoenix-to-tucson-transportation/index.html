Transportation Options Compared

Driving Yourself

  • Gas + wear on vehicle
  • Parking costs at destination
  • Fatigue from driving
  • Can't work or relax

Rideshare

  • High cost for long distance
  • Driver unfamiliarity with route
  • Variable quality
  • Surge pricing common

Professional Chauffeur

  • Fixed, predictable pricing
  • Experienced local drivers
  • Work or relax during travel
  • Premium vehicle comfort

Pricing

  • One-way transfer: quoted per trip. A transfer is flat within 15 road miles from pickup to drop-off; longer transfers are priced from the drive with the empty return included, so Tucson is priced from the drive
  • Round trip or day trip: hourly, $125 an hour in the Luxury SUV (up to 6) or $145 in the Executive Black Sprinter (up to 14), 4-hour minimum; 7+ hours earns 1 free hour

There are no sedans in our fleet. Prices are before 8% Arizona sales tax. Every rate is on the pricing page.
