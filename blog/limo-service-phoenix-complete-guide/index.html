Frequently Asked Questions

Is Phoenix limo service only the Chrysler Limo?
No. Most groups book a Sprinter or a party bus, because those cabins hold the people and the bags the Chrysler does not. The party bus rental is the night-out cabin. The Executive Sprinter is the forward-facing one.

Do you pick up at Sky Harbor?
Yes. Sky Harbor and Scottsdale Airport are one-way transfers. The Executive Sprinter one-way is $295 inside the flat radius. Farther drop-offs are priced from the drive, with the empty return included. Tell us the flight and the address.

Can guests bring drinks?
Passengers 21 and over may bring their own. We do not sell alcohol. The chauffeur does not drink, and if anyone under 21 is aboard the trip is alcohol-free.

Where are the hourly rates?
Hourly rates are on the pricing page. Gratuity is optional and is not added for you. Checkout shows exactly what is charged today and when any balance is due.
