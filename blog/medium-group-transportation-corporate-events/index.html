Medium Group Transportation for Corporate Events

Ideal Vehicle Options

Mini Bus (24 Pax) and Executive Mini Coach (29 Pax)

The Goldilocks option for medium groups

Features:

  • Comfortable high-back seating
  • Luggage differs by unit: the Mini Bus (24 Pax) takes limited luggage, and the Executive Mini Coach (29 Pax) has room for about 30 bags
  • Any other facility you need is confirmed in writing before you book
  • Climate control
  • Professional driver

Best for: Team outings, client dinners, conference shuttles

Multiple Executive Sprinters (14 each)

Flexible convoy approach

Benefits:

  • Can split and reunite groups
  • Different drop-off locations
  • More intimate setting
  • Easier parking at destinations

Best for: Roadshows, golf outings, multi-venue events

Planning Considerations

Headcount Accuracy

  • Confirm final numbers 48 hours before
  • Build in 10% buffer for last-minute additions
  • Communicate no-shows to avoid paying for unused capacity

Timing

  • Allow 15 minutes for boarding
  • Factor in Phoenix traffic patterns
  • Build buffer for multi-stop routes

Comfort

  • Limit mini coach trips to 2-3 hours
  • Provide refreshments on longer journeys
  • Plan restroom stops on longer journeys
