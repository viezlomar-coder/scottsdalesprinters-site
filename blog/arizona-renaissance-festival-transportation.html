Arizona Renaissance Festival Transportation: Journey to the Faire

Scottsdale Sprinters is a Scottsdale chauffeured transportation company serving metro Phoenix. Transport yourself to medieval times at the Arizona Renaissance Festival. Travel in comfort to this beloved annual celebration of renaissance culture and entertainment.

Arizona Renaissance Festival Overview

Running February 6 to April 4, 2027 — weekends plus Monday February 15, 10 AM to 6 PM — in Gold Canyon, the Ren Faire features:

  • 16 stages of continuous entertainment
  • 200+ artisan shops
  • Jousting tournaments
  • Turkey legs and mead
  • Costumed performers and guests

Getting There

Location

The festival grounds are at 12601 E. US Highway 60 in Gold Canyon, seven miles east of Apache Junction, reached via US-60 East (official FAQ, checked 2026-09-15).

Parking Reality

Festival parking involves:

  • Dirt lot parking, and a walk to the entrance
  • Free, per the festival's official FAQ (checked 2026-09-15)
  • Leaving at closing takes as long as it takes; a chauffeur waits with the vehicle

Transportation Advantages

Costume-Friendly

Arrive in your renaissance attire without dust, wrinkles, or awkward car maneuvering. Our vehicles accommodate elaborate costumes with care.

Group Celebrations

Creature Comforts

After hours of walking the grounds, return to climate-controlled comfort. Cold water, phone charging, and relaxation await.
