Why Scottsdale for Bachelor Parties?

The Stats That Matter

  • Sunny days: about 300 a year
  • Golf courses: hundreds across the metro
  • Nightlife: dozens of venues in Old Town alone
  • Perfect temps: March-May and September-November
  • Flight access: Sky Harbor serves everywhere

What Makes It Work

  • Everything is concentrated; Old Town is walkable
  • World-class yet unpretentious
  • Outdoor adventures are close by
  • Pool culture is thriving
  • Vegas energy without the casino commitment

Best Time to Plan Your Scottsdale Bachelor Party

Prime Season (October-May)

Weather: perfect, 60-85°F days and cool nights
Crowds: moderate to busy
Booking: availability varies by date; request a current quote

Shoulder Season (June, September)

Weather: hot but manageable
Crowds: lighter

Summer (July-August)

Weather: brutal outdoors, 105-115°F
Crowds: lightest of the year
Note: everything indoors still works: pools, clubs, casinos

Scottsdale Bachelor Party Itineraries

The Classic Weekend (2 nights)

Friday

  • 4:00 PM: Check into the house or hotel
  • 5:00 PM: Pool time
  • 7:30 PM: Dinner at STK Scottsdale
  • 10:00 PM: Old Town crawl
  • 2:00 AM: Late-night tacos at Taco Guild

Saturday

  • 8:00 AM: Golf at TPC Scottsdale or Grayhawk (book weeks ahead)
  • 1:00 PM: Lunch at Orange Sky (Talking Stick)
  • 3:00 PM: Pool party at W Scottsdale or Talking Stick
  • 7:00 PM: Back to the house, clean up
  • 8:30 PM: Dinner at Dominick's Steakhouse
  • 10:30 PM: VIP table at an Old Town club
  • 2:00 AM: Groom's choice

Sunday

  • 10:00 AM: Brunch at Hash Kitchen
  • 12:00 PM: Topgolf or the shooting range
  • 3:00 PM: Flights home

Transportation: Party Bus (28 Pax) on Friday night (5 hours, $1,250) and Saturday night (6 hours, $1,500), plus an Executive Sprinter to and from golf (6 hours, $870). For 20 guys the bus nights are about $63 and $75 per person, and the golf Sprinter split 12 ways is about $73 per person.

The Adventure Weekend (3 nights)

Thursday: arrive, pool, casual dinner at The Montauk, low-key Old Town warm-up

Friday

  • Morning: Sonoran Desert ATV tour, about 3 hours
  • Afternoon: pool recovery
  • Evening: dinner at Mastro's Ocean Club
  • Night: full Old Town deployment (party bus recommended)

Saturday

  • Morning: golf at Troon North
  • Afternoon: float the Salt River
  • Evening: dinner at Ocean 44
  • Night: an Old Town club

Sunday: brunch at The Henry, cigars and whiskey at Steak 44, departures

The Budget Banger (2 nights)

Yes, you can do Scottsdale without breaking the bank:

Lodging: a rental house with a pool, split eight or ten ways
Golf: municipal courses instead of resort courses
Dinner: skip the steakhouses; hit a gastropub or a pizza-and-beer room
Nightlife: pre-game at the house, skip bottle service
Transport: split a Limo Party Sprinter (16 Pax) for 5 hours, $875, about $55 per person for 16 or about $73 for 12

Top Bachelor Party Activities in Scottsdale

Golf

Championship courses: TPC Scottsdale (Stadium), Grayhawk (Talon and Raptor), Troon North (Monument and Pinnacle), Quintero
Value courses: Papago, McCormick Ranch, Stonecreek, Starfire
Tee times: the top courses take reservations further out than the value courses

Outdoor Adventures

  • Desert ATV or UTV tours (3-4 hours)
  • Salt River float trip (bring a cooler)
  • Shooting range at Scottsdale Gun Club
  • Skydiving in Eloy, south along I-10
  • Sunrise hot-air balloon

Pool Parties

  • Talking Stick Resort: biggest pool complex, DJs on weekends
  • W Scottsdale: see-and-be-seen
  • The Saguaro: colorful and casual
  • Mountain Shadows: relaxed and upscale

Reserve daybeds ahead on Saturdays.

Nightlife

High energy: the club block on Scottsdale Road
Lounge vibes: the quieter rooms off Stetson Drive and Craftsman Court
Live music: the country and rock rooms near Main Street

Other Activities

Driving ranges, indoor karting, bowling and arcades, and a casino night.

Scottsdale Bachelor Party FAQs

How far in advance should we book?
Availability varies by date and by vehicle. Request a current quote for your date and we will confirm what is open. Request a current quote for the transportation; lodging, tee times and nightlife tables are arranged with those venues.

What is the ideal group size?
Ten to fourteen. Big enough to party, small enough to move efficiently and get restaurant tables.

Suits or casual?
Upscale casual. Nice jeans, button-downs or polos. Clubs may bar athletic wear and shorts.

Should we get a VIP table?
Worth it with 8 or more people who want a reserved spot. Without one, expect lines and a crowded floor.

Can we really golf and party on the same day?
Yes, but respect the timeline: golf ends 1-2 PM, pool 3-6 PM, dinner 8 PM, clubs 10 PM on. Hydrate.
