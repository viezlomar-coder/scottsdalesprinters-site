Party Bus Safety Tips: Have Fun While Staying Safe

Scottsdale Sprinters is a Scottsdale chauffeured transportation company serving metro Phoenix. Essential safety guidelines for party bus celebrations. Maximize fun while keeping everyone secure.

Before the Party

Verify the Company

  • Confirm commercial insurance
  • Check DOT licensing
  • Read recent reviews
  • Ask about driver screening

Plan Your Trip

  • Share itinerary with someone not attending
  • Confirm pickup/drop-off locations
  • Establish communication with driver
  • Know emergency contacts

On the Bus

General Safety

  • Stay seated while moving
  • Keep aisles clear
  • Don't distract the driver
  • Follow driver instructions

Alcohol Responsibility

  • Pace yourself
  • Eat before and during
  • Hydrate with water
  • Watch out for friends

What NOT to Do

  • Don't hang out windows
  • Don't stand on seats
  • Don't throw items
  • Don't bring illegal substances

Group Management

Designate a Leader

One sober (or less intoxicated) person should:

  • Coordinate with driver
  • Keep track of time
  • Handle any issues
  • Ensure everyone returns

Head Counts

Before departing each stop:

  • Count your group
  • Confirm everyone is aboard
  • Check for left belongings
  • Verify next destination

Emergency Preparedness

Know the Basics

  • Bus emergency exits
  • Driver's phone number
  • Your location at all times
  • Venue addresses

If Issues Arise

  • Communicate with driver first
  • Call transportation company
  • Contact 911 if emergency
  • Document any incidents

Common Sense Rules

  • Respect the vehicle
  • Tip well for good service
  • Don't overcrowd beyond capacity
  • Leave the bus as you found it
