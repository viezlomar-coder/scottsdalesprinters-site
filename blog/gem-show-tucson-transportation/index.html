Scottsdale Sprinters is a Scottsdale chauffeured transportation company serving metro Phoenix. The Tucson Gem, Mineral & Fossil Showcase draws 50,000+ buyers and enthusiasts from around the world. Navigate this sprawling event efficiently with professional transportation.

Tucson Gem Show Overview

Running January through February, the Tucson Gem Show encompasses:

  • 45+ individual shows across the city
  • Convention centers, hotels, and outdoor venues
  • International dealers and rare specimens
  • Wholesale and retail opportunities

Transportation Challenges

Venue Sprawl

Unlike a single convention, the Gem Show spans Tucson:

  • Main Convention Center downtown
  • Hotels along I-10 corridor
  • Outdoor tent shows in multiple areas
  • Specialty shows 15-20 miles apart

Navigation Complexity

First-time attendees struggle with:

  • Finding specific venues
  • Parking at each location
  • Managing purchases between stops
  • Fatigue from constant driving
